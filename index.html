Privacy Policy

FaceForward · Last updated: 21 November 2026

The short version: FaceForward works offline and never sends your exercises, photos or settings anywhere. The only data that leaves your device comes from the advertising the app shows.

1. Data the app stores on your device

The app has no account and no server. Everything you enter is stored locally on your device in the app's private storage:

  • the answers you give during the onboarding (braces, breathing, jaw issues, posture),
  • the exercises you marked as done and the daily log,
  • the progress photos you take,
  • your settings (reminders, theme).

This data is not transmitted by the app, is not shared with us, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the app data from the Android settings.

2. Advertising

The app shows ads that are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners use the device's advertising ID and information about the device and the ad request, such as the IP address and the app version.

  • Personalized ads – only when you have given consent (see section 3). Google may then use the advertising ID to show ads that are more relevant to you.
  • Non-personalized ads – where consent was not given, the app only requests non-personalized ads, which are selected without using the advertising ID for personalization.

You can also remove the ads for 24 hours by watching a short video in the app's settings.

Google's own privacy policy applies to the ads: policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Consent (User Messaging Platform)

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where the law requires it, a consent form is shown before personalized ads are served. The form appears only when it is required and when the app is about to show ads – it never blocks the onboarding and never appears during your first session.

If you want to change your choice later, clear the app's data or reinstall the app: the consent form is then shown again the next time ads are about to be served.

4. Permissions

  • Camera – only to take a progress photo, and only when you start it.
  • Photos – only to pick a progress photo from your gallery, and only when you start it.
  • Notifications – only for the reminders you switch on in the settings.
  • Internet – used by the advertising SDK to load ads. The app itself does not use the network.

5. Children

The app is aimed at users aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and the ads are not child-directed.

6. Your rights

Because the app stores its data only on your device, you can delete everything by uninstalling the app or by clearing its data in the Android settings. You can also reset the advertising ID in the Android settings ("Privacy → Ads", or the "Advertising ID" option) and delete it from there.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy or about your data: aspireapps4u@gmail.com

8. Changes

This policy may change when the app changes. The date at the top of this page shows the last change.

FaceForward is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any medical provider, and the ads it shows are not medical advice.
